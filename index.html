Consultants in planning, engineering and research

Integrated Engineering, Planning and Research for Development

Nepal Development Consultancy (NDC) is a multidisciplinary consulting firm based in Nepal, specializing in engineering, urban and rural planning, GIS and spatial analysis, ecosystem-based adaptation, and socio-economic and environmental research. We work with government agencies, development partners, INGOs, and other institutions to provide evidence-based, practical solutions that advance sustainable development, strengthen infrastructure planning, and support resilient communities across Nepal.
