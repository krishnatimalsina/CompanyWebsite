Our Services

Nepal Development Consultancy provides engineering, planning, research, and spatial analysis services to support sustainable infrastructure, informed decision-making, and resilient communities across Nepal.

Engineering and Infrastructure

We provide engineering surveys, feasibility studies, designs, and detailed project reports for infrastructure development. Our experience includes transport facilities, water supply, irrigation, drainage, and public buildings.

  • Roads, bus parks, and transportation infrastructure
  • Water supply, irrigation, drainage, and sanitation systems
  • Engineering surveys, designs, cost estimates, and DPRs

Socio-economic Research and Studies

We conduct surveys, assessments, and applied research to understand local needs and support evidence-based development. Our work covers household conditions, livelihoods, agriculture, food security, and social protection.

  • Household surveys and socio-economic assessments
  • Agriculture, food security, and nutrition studies
  • Livelihoods, vulnerability, and social protection research

Urban and Regional Planning

We help municipalities and development agencies prepare practical plans for organized settlement, urban growth, and long-term development. Our project experience includes integrated urban development plans, smart city master plans, and risk-sensitive land use planning.

  • Integrated urban, rural, and regional development plans
  • Smart city master plans and new town development
  • Land use, settlement, transport, and periodic plans

Environmental Management

We support environmentally responsible development through environmental studies, natural resource assessments, and climate adaptation research. Our work helps identify potential impacts and inform sustainable planning and resource use.

  • Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) studies
  • Environmental profiles and natural resource planning
  • Climate change adaptation and water resource studies

GIS and Spatial Analysis

We use geographic information systems, mapping, and spatial data to support land use planning, infrastructure development, and resource management. Our experience includes municipal base maps, urban maps, road inventories, and risk-sensitive land use planning.

  • GIS-based municipal maps and spatial databases
  • Land use mapping, road inventories, and urban mapping
  • Spatial analysis and risk-sensitive land use planning
