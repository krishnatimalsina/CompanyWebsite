About us

Who we are, what we believe and the people behind the work.

Who we are

Nepal Development Consultancy (NDC) is a multidisciplinary consulting firm in Nepal, bringing together professionals from engineering, urban planning, GIS and mapping, environmental science, and social science. We work on a wide range of development projects, from infrastructure surveys and engineering design to land use planning, environmental studies, and socio-economic research. Our team combines technical knowledge with local understanding to develop practical solutions that respond to Nepal’s needs.

We believe that sustainable development begins with understanding local challenges and working together to address them. Our work includes feasibility studies, surveys, development planning, project assessment, engineering design, and technical support for both government and non-government organizations. We are committed to delivering reliable, research-based services that help improve infrastructure, strengthen communities, and support informed decision-making. Through our work, we aim to contribute to a more sustainable, resilient, and prosperous Nepal for future generations.

Our mission

To provide practical and sustainable solutions that improve lives and support Nepal’s development.

Our vision

To help build a more sustainable, resilient, and prosperous Nepal for future generations.

Our values

Sustainable Development

Promoting responsible planning and resource management to build a sustainable future for Nepal.

Resilience and Disaster Risk Reduction

Strengthening communities and infrastructure through risk-informed planning, disaster preparedness, and climate adaptation.

Accountability and Transparency

Working honestly and responsibly, with clear communication and a commitment to delivering quality work.

Community Well-being

Putting people first and supporting projects that improve livelihoods and create better opportunities for communities across Nepal.
